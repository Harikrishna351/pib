Civic Timeline

June 4, 2023 | 6:30 AM Incident

Aguwani-Sultanganj Bridge Collapse

Under-construction bridge across Burhi Gandak River collapses for the second time. The ₹1,710 crore project, meant to connect Saran and Bhagalpur districts, had partially collapsed in April 2022. This time, the entire superstructure falls into the river during construction.

Source: PIB, The Hindu, NDTV
June 4, 2023 | 2:00 PM Govt Response

CM Nitish Kumar Orders Probe

Bihar Chief Minister announces high-level committee under Special Secretary of Building Construction Department. Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) seeks report within 15 days. SP Company (contractor) blacklisted from future projects.

Source: Chief Minister's Office, Bihar
June 5-10, 2023 Public Response

Local Protests & Opposition Demands

Residents block NH-31 demanding accountability. Opposition parties RJD and Congress stage protests in Patna. PIL filed in Patna High Court seeking CBI inquiry into corruption allegations in construction quality.

Source: Local stringers, High Court Registry
June 27, 2023 Policy Change

Bihar Infrastructure Safety Audit Ordered

State government mandates structural audit of all under-construction bridges in Bihar. Third-party inspection mandatory for all projects above ₹50 crore. New clauses added to contractor agreements: "Total liability for design failure."

Source: Cabinet Decision, Bihar Govt
August 14, 2023 Investigation

Forensic Report Submitted

IIT-Roorkee forensic report identifies design flaws and substandard materials. Report confirms use of inferior quality cement and inadequate welding of joints. Recommends dismantling remaining structure.

Source: IIT Roorkee (Confidential, leaked to press)
March 2024 Current Status

Case Under Investigation, No Reconstruction

EOW (Economic Offences Wing) arrests 3 including project manager and quality control engineer. Project cost escalated by ₹400 crore for demolition and reconstruction. Tender process delayed due to contractor blacklisting.

Source: EOW Bihar, PTI

What Went Wrong

  • Design flaws identified by IIT-Roorkee report
  • Substandard cement used (quality 30% below spec)
  • Inadequate supervision by state engineers
  • Previous collapse warning (April 2022) ignored
  • Contractor continued work without design revision
  • No third-party audit before second collapse

Preventive Measures Now Active

  • Mandatory third-party inspection for >₹50Cr projects
  • Real-time monitoring sensors on bridge structures
  • Contractor liability extended to 10 years
  • Whistleblower protection for quality engineers
  • Drone surveillance of construction sites
  • Centralized database of blacklisted contractors
